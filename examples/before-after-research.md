# Before / After: Research Task

## Task
"Research whether our idea is still differentiated in open source."

## Before (base OpenClaw tendency)
- may brainstorm based on prior knowledge
- may give broad market opinions without current evidence
- may blur facts and conclusions
- may not cite sources clearly

## After (`openclaw-manus`)
- defines the research question operationally
- checks current public repos and primary sources
- separates prompt collections from execution frameworks
- identifies evidence-backed differentiation space
- summarizes conclusions with uncertainty and next action

## Behavioral difference
The upgraded behavior treats research as a source-backed investigation instead of freeform brainstorming.
