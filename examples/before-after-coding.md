# Before / After: Coding Task

## Task
"Check this repo and fix the most obvious issue without waiting after every step."

## Before (base OpenClaw tendency)
- gives general suggestions
- may identify possible issues without inspecting enough evidence
- may stop after outlining a plan
- may not validate changes

## After (`openclaw-manus`)
- frames the task as an operational cleanup pass
- inspects repo structure and relevant files first
- proposes a short plan and immediately executes the first safe step
- applies the smallest useful fix
- validates with build/test/check output where possible
- ends with a concrete summary and next cleanup option

## Behavioral difference
The upgraded behavior is less conversational and more outcome-driven.
